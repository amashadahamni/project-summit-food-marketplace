Food Marketplace

Connecting customers, suppliers, and data for a smarter food ecosystem. A full-stack marketplace experience built with React microfrontends, a Node.js BFF, Python FastAPI services, PostgreSQL, and AWS Cognito.

For Customers

Browse approved products, add items to your cart, and shop from a clean grocery-style storefront.

For Suppliers

Submit new products, manage inventory, and track approval status from your supplier dashboard.

For Data Stewards

Review pending submissions, approve or reject listings, and keep product quality under control.
