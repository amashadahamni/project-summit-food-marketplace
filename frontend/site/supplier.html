Supplier product management dashboard

Suppliers can submit listings as pending submissions, update price, stock, and descriptions, and manage only their own products through a role-specific workflow.

Submissions workflow

New listings go into pending review before they become visible to customers.

Own product control

Supplier users can update and deactivate only the products they submitted.

Approval status

Products are tracked with pending, approved, or rejected states and optional rejection feedback.

Supplier app features

This supplier view is designed to support inventory submissions and editable product details, while respecting the permissions boundary that prevents suppliers from managing other vendors' products.
