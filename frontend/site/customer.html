Customer shopping made simple

Browse approved products by category, search the marketplace by name, and manage one active cart at a time. This screen represents the customer-facing UI direction for the online grocery experience.

Approved items only

Customers only see products that have passed the supplier approval workflow.

Single active cart

Every customer can add products to one current cart and update or remove items easily.

Product details

Product listings include price, description, stock availability, and supplier metadata.

Customer app features

Customers will be able to browse categories such as Meats, Seafood, and Dairy, search products by name, view detailed cards, add approved items to cart, adjust quantities, and complete their shopping in a unified experience.

The final implementation will connect to the backend BFF and FastAPI services for live product availability and cart management.
